3. What the app does (and does not do)

moncycle.app is a cycle tracking notebook for natural methods (Billings, FertilityCare, with or without temperature). You record your observations; the App shows them as a chart and exports them (PDF, CSV, NFP).

It is a wellbeing tool, not a health service:

  • it is not a medical device, not a contraceptive, and not a diagnostic tool;
  • it does not interpret your observations and never tells you whether you are fertile or not: the marks on the chart are the ones you enter;
  • it replaces neither a healthcare professional nor the teaching of a method.

Training is required

The App assumes you have been trained in the natural method you use. Without that training, do not rely on it to make decisions. If you have trouble keeping or reading your chart, contact the association or organisation that teaches your method.

No method, app included, is 100% effective. For any medical question (pain, unusual bleeding, a late period, pregnancy, treatment), see a healthcare professional. In an emergency, call 112 (European Union) or 911 (United States).
